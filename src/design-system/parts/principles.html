01 Principles

A handful of rules. Each one has a test.

Five to seven rules, said plainly. Each settles a tension the product has (for example: energetic, yet calm enough to open ten times a day) and comes with a check anyone can run.

  1. One thing matters most on each screen.

    Tension
    Example — replace. Everything wants to be prominent.
    Settled
    Each screen names its one primary object or action; everything else steps back.
    Test
    Blur the screenshot by 12 px: the primary thing is still the first shape you see.
  2. The next rule: a name, its tension, how it is settled, its test.
