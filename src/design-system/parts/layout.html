04 Layout

One frame, a few fixed numbers.

Screens are drawn at 390 × 844. Bars, the gutter and touch targets are fixed numbers, shared by the wireframes and hi-fi, so a frame drawn in gray already has its final proportions.

WhatSizeRule
Frame390 × 844The design size; content reflows from 320 wide up.
Status bar47Top safe area; nothing tappable in it.
Nav bar44Back or close on the left, title centred, up to two actions.
Tab bar83 (49 + 34)Top-level destinations only; never with a pinned bar.
Pinned bar102 (16 + 52 + 34)The screen's one act (or Cancel + the act).
Gutter16Content's side margin; rows run edge to edge of it.
Touch target44 × 44 minEvery link and control, whatever it draws.
Button, field52 (small 40)Full width in the content; small hugs its label.
List row60 minHairlines between rows, not boxes around them.
Spacing4 gridTailwind's spacing scale (--spacing = 4 px).
