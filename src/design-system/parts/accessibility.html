06 Accessibility

The floor, not a feature.

These hold on every screen, in every theme. A design that can't meet one is redrawn, not excused.

Contrast

4.5 : 1 for text, 3 : 1 for large text, icons and control edges. Colour is never the only cue.

Targets

44 × 44 at least, for every link and control, whatever it draws.

Names

Icon-only controls carry a label; a toggle says its state (aria-pressed, aria-checked).

Text size

Layouts survive the largest text setting: lines wrap, rows grow, nothing clips.

Motion

Reduced motion keeps every state change, without the movement.

Focus

A visible focus ring on every focusable thing, on every surface.
