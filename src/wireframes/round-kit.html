Wireframes

Kit

The grayscale wireframe parts, src/components/wf/*.html — one part per file, its locals listed in its header comment — with their recipes in the /* wireframes */ block of src/styles.css. Geometry and hierarchy follow the Design System's Layout part, in gray only.

Screens are built from these parts; a layout that repeats becomes a part (project parts are named for what they are, package-specific ones prefixed with their package). Each board: the part, then the locals it was given.
